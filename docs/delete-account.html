Delete your MARCO account

This page explains how to request deletion of your MARCO Firebase account and associated cloud profile data.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  1. Open MARCO and open your account/profile menu.
  2. Choose Delete MARCO account and data.
  3. Confirm the request and complete the identity check. Email accounts must provide their current password; Google accounts must reauthenticate with Google.

The app deletes the Firebase user profile and the known settings/preferences document before deleting the Firebase Authentication account.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email xpangwan@gmail.com with the subject MARCO account deletion request.

Include the email address used for your MARCO account, or your Firebase UID if known. We may ask for reasonable proof that you own the account before processing the request. Never send your password, verification code, or Google access token.

Verified requests are handled within 30 days. You will receive a response if more information is needed or when processing is complete.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your Firestore profile document under users/{uid}.
  • The known Firestore settings/preferences document under that user profile.

What is not automatically deleted

  • Local game-library data, nickname, avatar, downloaded files, cached covers and device preferences. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local data from your device.
  • Blogger posts and Google Drive files uploaded by the separate administrator account. Their owner must delete those items directly in Blogger or Drive.
  • Information retained by Google/Firebase under their own legal, security, or backup retention obligations.

For more details, read the MARCO Privacy Policy.
