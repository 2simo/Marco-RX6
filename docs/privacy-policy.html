About this policy

This policy explains how the MARCO RX6 Android app handles information when you use its game-library, profile, account, notification, Blogger and Google Drive features. For privacy questions, contact xpangwan@gmail.com.

Information handled by the app

  • Firebase account information: when you use an account, Firebase Authentication processes an account identifier and the sign-in information supplied by your chosen provider. Email sign-up uses your email address; Google sign-in uses information returned by Google. The app can also create an anonymous Firebase guest identifier automatically.
  • Cloud profile and preferences: the app may store your Firebase UID, email address, display name, profile biography, profile photo URL, account type, and app preference values in your own Firestore user record.
  • On-device information: the game library, locally selected nickname and avatar, appearance and language preferences, notification preferences, cover cache and files you choose to download are stored locally on your device. These local items are not automatically uploaded as a single library to Firebase.
  • Google admin features: if you choose the separate administrator sign-in, the app requests Google access for Blogger and Google Drive file operations (Blogger and drive.file scopes). This is separate from your normal MARCO RX6 Firebase account. The app uses this access to manage Blogger posts and upload cover files to the administrator's Drive.
  • Public blog information: the app retrieves publicly available MARCO RX6 Blogger feed content to display and synchronize the game library.
  • Downloads and notifications: downloads are initiated by you from links associated with game entries. If enabled, scheduled notifications check the public MARCO RX6 Blogger feed over HTTPS for new posts.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, keep your profile and preferences synchronized, provide the app's library and profile features, support administrator-authorized Blogger and Drive operations, deliver notifications you enable, and carry out downloads you request.

Service providers and sharing

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are provided by Google Firebase and process the account and cloud profile information described above. Google Sign-In, Blogger and Drive are Google services used only when their corresponding features are invoked. The app does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by these providers under their own terms and privacy policies. Game download links may open third-party websites chosen by you; those sites have their own privacy practices.

Permissions and tracking

The app requests internet access for its online features and notification permission on supported Android versions when you enable notifications. The current app build does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. The app does not use advertising SDKs in the current build.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account and profile information is retained while the Firebase account exists, unless it is removed earlier or retention is required for security or legal reasons. You can delete your Firebase account from the account menu in the app, or submit an external request using the account deletion page. Verified email requests are handled within 30 days.

Deleting the Firebase account removes the app's Firebase user profile and known preferences document. It does not delete your local game library, downloads, cached covers, or local device preferences; clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove those local items. It also does not delete Blogger posts or files already uploaded to the administrator's Google Drive. Those Google-hosted items must be managed separately by their owner in Blogger or Drive.

Security

Cloud profile documents are scoped to the authenticated Firebase UID by the app's Firestore security rules. Network requests to the app's services use HTTPS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but access is limited to the app features and service providers described in this policy.

Children and changes

MARCO RX6 is a general game-library utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
